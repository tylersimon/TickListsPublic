# Tickly

**A simple checklist for shopping, packing and to-dos, on iPhone, Mac and Apple Watch.**

Make a list, tick things off, and they sink to the bottom so what's left is always at the top.

- Sections for each aisle, room or day
- Bold, colours and highlights to make things stand out
- Share lists to and from Notes, Messages and Mail
- Keep each list on your device or in your own iCloud
- Automatic backups to Files

Tickly collects no data: no accounts, no ads, no tracking.

## Supported devices

- iPhone with iOS 27 or later
- Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 27 or later
- Apple Watch with watchOS 26 or later, paired with an iPhone that has Tickly
